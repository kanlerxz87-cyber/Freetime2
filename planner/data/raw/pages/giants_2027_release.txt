 Giants announce tentative 2027 regular season schedule
 July 16th, 2026
 Share 
 SAN FRANCISCO, Calif. – The San Francisco Giants released their 2027 regular season schedule today, which will feature the Giants opening the season at home versus the Colorado Rockies on Thursday, March 25 th . It’ll mark the second consecutive season the Giants have opened the regular season at home and just the fourth time in the last 20 seasons (others: 2026 vs. New York-AL, 2009 vs. Milwaukee & 2022 vs. Miami).
 The Giants and Rockies will have an off day on Friday, March 26 th, before the series resumes Saturday, March 27 th, for game two, with the series finale slated for Sunday, March 28 th . After the three-game set against the Rockies, the Giants will host the Arizona Diamondbacks for three games (Monday, March 29 th -Wednesday, March 31 st ). Following the Giants’ opening homestand, the team will embark on a seven-game road trip to New York-NL (April 2 nd -4 th ) and Atlanta (April 5 th -8 th ).
 The Giants will host the Dodgers twice from June 14 th -16 th (three games) and August 6 th -8 th (three games), as well as the Boston Red Sox from August 24 th -26 th . The 2027 schedule also features home weekend series against the New York Mets (June 11 th -13 th ), Chicago Cubs (June 24 th -27 th ) and Athletics (July 16 th -18 th ). San Francisco has two 10-game homestands with the first against the San Diego Padres (May 24 th -27 th ), Cincinnati Reds (May 28 th -30 th ) and Washington Nationals (May 31 st -June 2 nd ) and the second against the Athletics (July 16 th -July 18 th ), Colorado Rockies (July 19 th -22 nd ) and San Diego Padres (July 23 rd -25 th ).
 For the first time since 2022, the Giants will finish the season on the road, traveling to the Minnesota Twins (September 21 st -September 23 rd ) and the Colorado Rockies (September 24 th -September 26 th ). The Giants’ final series of the season at Oracle Park will take place from September 17-19 against the Atlanta Braves.
 The Giants host the Colorado Rockies on Easter (March 28 th ) and the Washington Nationals on Memorial Day (May 31 st ). The team is in Los Angeles-NL for Mother’s Day (May 9 th ), Philadelphia for Father’s Day (June 20 th ), Arizona for Fourth of July (July 4 th ) and San Diego for Labor Day (September 6 th ).
