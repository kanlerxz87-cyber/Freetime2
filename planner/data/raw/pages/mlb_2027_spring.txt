 MLB announces 2027 Spring Training schedule
 All 30 Clubs to Open Spring Training Play on Friday, February 19th
 September 4th, 2026
 Share 
 Major League Baseball today announced that 2027 Spring Training will officially begin on Friday, February 19 th with all 30 Clubs in action across Arizona’s Cactus League and Florida’s Grapefruit League.
 A number of exhibition games from Saturday, March 20 th -Tuesday, March 23 rd  will be played at Major and Minor League ballparks. The scheduled meetings thus far include the Toronto Blue Jays visiting the Tampa Bay Rays at Tropicana Field in St. Petersburg on March 20 th ; the Detroit Tigers visiting the Rays at Tropicana Field on March 21 st  and March 23 rd ; the San Francisco Giants visiting the Triple-A Sacramento River Cats at Sutter Health Park in Sacramento on March 21 st ; the Los Angeles Dodgers hosting the Los Angeles Angels at Dodger Stadium in Los Angeles on March 21 st ; the Baltimore Orioles hosting the Washington Nationals at Camden Yards in Baltimore on March 21 st ; the Nationals hosting the Orioles at Nationals Park in Washington, D.C. on March 22 nd ; the Angels hosting the Dodgers at Angel Stadium in Anaheim on March 22 nd -23 rd ; the Giants hosting the Boston Red Sox at Oracle Park in San Francisco on March 22 nd -23 rd ; and the Houston Astros hosting the Triple-A Sugar Land Space Cowboys at Daikin Park in Houston on March 22 nd -23 rd . Additional exhibition games at Major and Minor League ballparks are expected to be added in the coming months.
 The 2027 regular season will begin with an Opening Night game presented by Adobe (matchup to be determined) live on Netflix on Wednesday, March 24 th , followed by a slate of 14 games played on traditional Opening Day presented by Adobe on Thursday, March 25 th . NBC will also begin its 2027 coverage with a primetime contest to be determined on Opening Day.
 Visit MLB.com for the complete 2027 Spring Training schedule  Individual Club schedules can be found at each of the respective 30 Club sites.
