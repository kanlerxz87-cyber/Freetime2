Executive Summary

Bottom line: The conservative season-window model finds no 7-day stretch in 2027, 2028, or 2029. Its longest shorter candidate is four days in each year; these are estimated shoulder gaps between NFL postseason and MLB spring training (or nearby bowl/postseason ranges), not verified conflict-free booking dates. What is verified

What is not verified
