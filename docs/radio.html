Who carries what, when

SF GIANTS (MLB)

KNBR 680 AM / 104.5 FM — flagship since 1979. Every Giants game, radio call with Dave Flemming. Also on KSAN 107.7 FM (The Bone). Spanish: KSFN? — not verified; check Giants broadcasts.

ATHLETICS (MLB)

KNEW 960 AM (iHeart Sports 960, Oakland, 5 kW) — Bay Area home since 2020. Sacramento flagship KSTE 650 AM (21.4 kW day) — weak in 94122; night power 920 W. Spanish: KIQI 1010 AM.

SF 49ERS (NFL)

KNBR 680/104.5 FM flagship (since the 1990s) + KSAN 107.7 FM + KSFO 810 AM. National games (TNF/SNF/MNF) air on the local call — Westwood One only reaches you when KNBR is not airing 49ers duty elsewhere (pre-emption rule).

STANFORD FOOTBALL

KNBR / KTCT 1050 AM — 2026 season on KTCT per gostanford.com (Richardson/Fisk). KTCT is a 50 kW Class B facility (10–35 kW at night, directional five-tower array, Hayward) — full-time, not a daytimer. Night games air fine in 94122, though daytime is strongest.

CAL FOOTBALL

KSFO 810 AM carried Nov 28, 2026 (Pittsburgh at Cal). Season assignment unverified per-game; check Cal’s page. Historic flagship was KNEW/KOFY; varies by year.

WESTWOOD ONE NATIONAL (NFL)

KNBR 680/104.5 (NFL + NCAAF affiliate in WW1 station finder). TNF ~5:15 PM PT, SNF ~5:20 PM PT, MNF ~5:15 PM PT, holiday tripleheaders. 49ers games stay on the local call.

Practical listening guidance for 94122

  • Everyday best bet: KNBR 680 AM. 50 kW clear-channel; indoors near a window it’s a reliable listen. 104.5 FM simulcast is the fidelity option.
  • FM sweet spots: 104.5 (KNBR), 107.7 (The Bone). Sutro Tower line-of-sight to 94122 is clean; FM is less weather-dependent than AM.
  • AM nightfall: 680 (clear channel) stays strong; 810 (ex-KGO 50 kW) holds well; 1050 drops to ~10–35 kW at night per Class B rules but remains audible; 650 (KSTE) fades hardest at night (920 W).
  • During 49ers games: you will hear the local KNBR crew — the Westwood One national window is pre-empted locally. To hear the WW1 national call you’d need an out-of-market affiliate or out-of-market stream (out of scope).
  • Out of scope but on the dial: KCBS 740 AM (news, some Warriors pre/post?), KGO 810 (news talk), 95.7 The Game (Warriors/Sharks, Audacy), KSJO? (oldies). None counted in our busy/free model.
