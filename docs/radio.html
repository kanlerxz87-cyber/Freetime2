Bay Area Radio — What you can hear on AM/FM in 94122

Primary: KNBR 680 AM / 104.5 FM — The Sports Leader (San Francisco)

  • KNBR 680 AM (50,000-watt clear-channel, Redwood City transmitter) + KNBR-FM 104.5 simulcast (Sutro Tower) — both receivable on a standard AM/FM radio in Outer Sunset.
  • Westwood One Sports affiliate — carries Westwood One national NFL radio (TNF/MNF/SNF, holiday tripleheaders, playoffs, Super Bowl).
  • Giants Radio Network flagship (since 1979) and 49ers Radio Network co-flagship (with KSAN/KSFO); also Stanford Cardinal radio home per station materials.
  • Official programming/schedule: thesportsleader.com/shows • Westwood One NFL schedule: westwoodonesports.com/nfl-schedule
