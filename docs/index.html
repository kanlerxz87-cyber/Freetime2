DASHBOARD · QUESTION-FIRST

When can I actually leave and miss nothing?

Short answer: almost never, if you insist on missing everything — MLB alone is on the air nearly every day from February to October. The defensible answer is the January–February lull: strict islands of 4–11 days (February gap between Super Bowl and Spring Training), or a ~40-day practical run (Jan → mid-Feb) when you only care about Bay Area/national broadcasts.
