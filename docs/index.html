Dashboard — When are you free?

Question: When is the best time to take a vacation from Outer Sunset (94122) without missing live sports you would stay home to hear on AM/FM radio?

Short answer: Under the strict definition (any of all 30 MLB clubs incl. Spring Training, any NFL game, Stanford/Cal, Westwood One national), the only multi-day gap each year is a short February window between the Super Bowl and Spring Training — about 11 days in 2026 (Feb 9–19) and only ~4 days in 2027–2029 (estimated). A true 2–3 week vacation requires the practical definition (Giants/A's/49ers/Stanford/Cal/Westwood One only, ignoring other clubs' Spring Training): mid-February to late March. See Vacation Windows.
