Executive Summary

Bottom line

There is no 2–3 week fully-free window under the strict all-30-clubs definition. MLB (Spring Training Feb–Mar through postseason Oct), NFL (preseason Aug through Super Bowl Feb), and Stanford/Cal NCAAF (late Aug through bowls/CFP Jan) overlap or abut almost year-round. The only strict gap is in February between the Super Bowl and Spring Training.

What counts as busy

  • MLB: Giants + Athletics (high priority) + all 30 clubs, Spring Training through World Series.
  • NFL: 49ers (high priority) + all NFL games; Westwood One national radio games are a subset of NFL dates.
  • NCAAF: Stanford + Cal (high priority), regular season through bowls/CFP.
  • Radio: KNBR 680 AM / 104.5 FM (Westwood One Sports affiliate) is the primary Bay Area outlet; see Radio page for others.

Daily free time

On game days, free hours = before first game / between games / after last game (PT), using researched averages: MLB ~170 min, NFL ~192 min, NCAAF ~204 min. Example: 1:05 PM PT NFL kickoff → ~4:17 PM PT expected end. Use the Day Planner for exact math per date.
