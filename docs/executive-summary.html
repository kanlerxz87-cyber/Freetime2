There is no 2–3 week fully-free window. February is the only real break.

Strict (all 30 MLB clubs + all NFL + Stanford/Cal): every year the only genuinely quiet span is the Super Bowl → MLB Spring Training gap in February — 4–11 days, not weeks. Practical (Giants/A’s, 49ers, Stanford/Cal, Westwood One only): ~38–44 days in January–February each year, before Opening Night. Multi-week 1–3-week windows otherwise don’t exist: MLB (Feb–Oct), NFL (Sep–Feb), NCAAF (Aug–Jan) overlap year-round.

Why not other windows?

  • Summer (June–August): MLB every single day; NFL preseason starts mid-August; NCAAF practice camps air; not feasible.
  • Late April/May: MLB daily + NBA/NHL (out of scope but on the dial) + soccer (out of scope). MLB alone kills it strictly.
  • November–December: NFL every weekend + Thanksgiving/Christmas; MLB ended but football floods the dial. A few midweek free days exist; no runs of 7+.
  • Early January: Wild Card weekend is jammed; divisional/championship rounds next. The playoff weeks are among the busiest days of the year.
  • The February window: between Super Bowl Sunday and the first spring-training exhibitions. ~4–11 strict days; ~38–44 practical days. This is the entire opportunity.
