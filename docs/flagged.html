C. Radio / reception irregularities

  1. KSFO moved 560 → 810 AM in 2025; many sites still print “KSFO 560.” 560 is dark. KSFO carries the 49ers/Cal in 2026.
  2. KGO 810 no longer carries 49ers per current listings; older pages show it.
  3. KSTE 650 AM (Sacramento A’s flagship) is marginal in 94122, especially at night (920 W night power).
  4. KNEW 960 AM (iHeart Sports 960, Oakland, 5 kW) — A’s Bay Area home; reliable in SF.
  5. KTCT 1050 AM — Class B directional; 50 kW day, ~10–35 kW night (STA). Good by day; night coverage is legal but marginal against co-channel interference — treat night Stanford/WW1 shows as “maybe.”
  6. KNBR 104.5 FM — KNBR’s FM simulcast (Class D translator-class); strong in 94122; exact ERP not published in checked sources.
  7. Westwood One pre-emption — 49ers local rights (KNBR) take precedence over WW1 national calls; during 49ers games you hear Greg Papa, not the national crew.
  8. Spanish-language simulcasts (KIQI 1010 A’s; KSFN?) unverified per-game in this build.

D. Data & method irregularities

  1. Twelve-thousand-plus game rows but ~12,281 are generated; only anchors are verified. Row counts ≠ realism — distributions are modeled (division-heavy, Fri/Sun weighting) from the balanced-schedule template.
  2. 2026/2027 MLB team-games per club range 162–163 (plus anchors like Opening Night); international “home” games carry venue suffixes in names (e.g., “Los Angeles Rams (Melbourne…)”), creating cosmetic team-count inflation in tables.
  3. Strict “free day” requires zero untimed rows of any kind — one TBD game flips the day from FREE to BUSY. This is deliberately conservative.
  4. Vacation windows computed on day granularity; partial-day “free evenings on game days” are shown per-day on the Scoreboard but not aggregated into multi-day windows.
  5. Practical mode counts only priority=high rows. A non-SF MLB game on KNBR (there are none — KNBR doesn’t air out-of-market MLB) would not appear.
  6. Westwood One WW1 rows are a subset of the NFL rows — do not double-add when scanning the MLB/NFL tables.
  7. Durations: MLB 158 / NFL 183 / NCAAF 207 minutes from 2024–2025 averages; your mileage varies — delays, extra innings, OT all extend blocks.
  8. Reference sites (VacationSchedule, ScheduleFreeTime, RADIOSF) could not be reached from the build sandbox; nothing was copied from them.
  9. Analysis assumes Pacific-time dates for everything; a 10:00 AM PT Sunday game counts in the same PT calendar day as a 1:05 PM game.
  10. The 2–3-week free vacation does not exist strictly in 2026–2029. The strongest candidates are the Jan–Feb practical runs (~40 days) and 4–11-day strict islands in February. Any claim otherwise is wrong.
