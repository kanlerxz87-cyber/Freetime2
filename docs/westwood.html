Bay Area reception notes (outer Sunset 94122)

  • KNBR 680 AM / 104.5 FM — 50 kW clear-channel AM; Class A; 104.5 FM simulcast. Loudest, clearest sports signal in SF. Westwood One NFL affiliate. Also Giants + 49ers flagship.
  • KSAN 107.7 FM (“The Bone”) — 100 kW equivalent ERP; 49ers/Giants secondary. Reliable FM in the Richmond/Sunset.
  • KSFO 810 AM — conservative talk; 50 kW. Carried 49ers and Cal football (Nov 28, 2026). Now at 810 AM since 2025 move from 560 (560 is dark).
  • KNEW 960 AM — iHeart “iHeart Sports 960”; Athletics Bay Area home (vs Sacramento flagship KSTE 650, marginal in SF).
  • KTCT 1050 AM — 50 kW Class B directional (Hayward), 10–35 kW at night; Stanford football flagship (2026); Westwood One affiliate. Full-time but listen strongest by day.
  • KIQI 1010 AM — A’s Spanish-language home; simulcasts select games.
