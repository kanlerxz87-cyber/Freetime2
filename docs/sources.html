Durations are planning estimates, not predictions. First pitch / kickoff to final out is what blocks “free” time here; pregame and postgame radio shows are out of scope unless noted.

Not verifiable / not included

  • Game-by-game MLB rows for 2026/2027 (released but not machine-readable in this environment) — ESTIMATED reconstruction used; see MLB page and Flagged.
  • All MLB/NFL/NCAAF rows for 2028–2029 — do not exist yet (2028 MLB expected ~mid-2027; NFL ~May 2027).
  • Local pre-emption of Westwood One windows by 49ers/Giants carriage conflicts — KNBR airs Westwood One subject to local rights; not programmatically decidable here.
  • NBA/NHL/MLS/NWSL/USFL/softball etc. on Bay Area AM/FM — out of scope by design; would shrink windows further (noted for completeness).
  • KNEW 960 vs KIQI 1010 Spanish-language simulcast consistency — unverified per game.
  • KTCT 1050 night pattern — 10–35 kW at night under STA/class rules; interference protection for co-channel Class A (XEG Monterrey, WBAL) makes night reception conditional; verify by listening at the actual venue.
