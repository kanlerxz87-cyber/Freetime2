How it works

Method & sources.

What counts

Relevant live games occupy time continuously, including halftime. The scope also includes other local live radio sports; that station survey remains incomplete.

Time zone

Times are Pacific local time: PST in winter and PDT during daylight saving. Westwood One Eastern times were converted to Pacific at a three-hour offset for these listed dates.

Uncertainty

A day is free only when all relevant schedules and carriage have been checked. This snapshot does not meet that test for any day.
